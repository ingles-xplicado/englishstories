A New Best Friend

On Saturday morning, Emily decided to visit the local animal shelter. She had been thinking about getting a dog for months, but she wasn't sure what kind she wanted. When she arrived, she saw dogs of all different sizes. Some were barking, some were sleeping, and others were jumping around excitedly. Then she noticed a small brown dog sitting quietly in the corner of his cage. His name was Charlie, and he was three years old. Emily asked one of the workers if she could spend some time with him. At first, Charlie was a little shy, but after a few minutes, he started wagging his tail and licking her hand. Emily immediately fell in love with him. She filled out the adoption paperwork, bought a leash, and took Charlie home that afternoon. When they got home, Charlie ran around the living room, sniffed everything, and eventually fell asleep on the couch. Emily smiled and said, "I think you're going to like it here, buddy." For the first time in a long time, Charlie finally had a family of his own.
